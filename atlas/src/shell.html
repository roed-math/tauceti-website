How the atlas works

  1. Galaxies are roadmaps, grouped into mathematical areas. Zoom towards a galaxy, or click it, to enter.
  2. Stars are layers. Inside a roadmap each star is a mathematical topic; arrows point from prerequisite to consumer. Zoom into a star or click it.
  3. Planets are the mathematics. A layer's definitions, theorems and constructions orbit it. Select a planet to read its statement and references.

Scroll or pinch to zoom and drag to pan. Zooming out retraces your route, as does the back control above the map. Your browser's back button also works. Press ⌘K or Ctrl K to search.

Colours

Green, amber and red mark layers recorded as complete, in progress or not started. Layers with no recorded status are left uncoloured and are counted under “no progress data” in Mission status; they are never shown as started or as zero percent. Dashed outlines mark areas that have no roadmap yet.

Connections

The overview keeps only the strongest links between areas visible. Hover an area, or select a roadmap, to see its own prerequisite links; the Links tab lists the layer pairs behind each one. Related-plan links can be switched on and carry no prerequisite claim.

The roadmap texts are embedded, so the page works offline. Administrative tasks stay in the full source and in the totals but are not drawn as stars. Progress changes and pins are saved in this browser only and never edit the Tau Ceti repositories. Recorded statuses are dated snapshots.

Your progress

Open a layer star, choose its status in the details panel and add a note. Updates change its colour and are saved locally. “Use recorded status” removes an override.

An imported file restores local updates. Recorded source reports stay embedded separately. These controls never edit the Tau Ceti repositories.

Counts use the smallest tracked layers; parent headings and collections are counted once. “Complete” means recorded complete or marked complete in this browser. Source reports are dated snapshots, not a fresh verification of the code. Layers without recorded status stay in the totals and have no colour on the map.
