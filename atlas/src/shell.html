FIND YOUR WAY

Follow the mathematics inward.

  1. Galaxies are roadmaps. Zoom towards a galaxy or click it to enter. Its mathematical scope and connections appear in the details panel.
  2. Stars are mathematical topics. Inside a roadmap, the mathematical layers form a constellation. Zoom towards a star or click it to explore that layer and its orbiting planets.
  3. Planets hold the mathematics. Select a planet to read a definition, theorem or construction from the layer. The full roadmap remains available for context.

Scroll to zoom and drag the background to pan. Zoom out to retrace your route, or use the navigation buttons above the scene. “Fit” brings the current view back into frame. Your browser’s Back and Forward buttons also move between views.

Read the connections

Links between galaxies show recorded prerequisites. Hover or select an object to highlight its immediate connections; a roadmap’s Links tab explains the layer-level relationships. Optional dotted links show related mathematics. Search and Subjects offer direct routes to a topic.

Follow the progress

Red, amber and green accents show layer progress. A roadmap’s percentage counts its completed tracked leaf layers. Layers without recorded status have no progress label or status colour. Planets describe the mathematics; progress is recorded on their parent layer.

Dashed grey areas identify subjects without a dedicated roadmap in this atlas. They are excluded from completion totals. Use Mission status for the breakdown, and pin useful roadmaps from their details.

Full roadmap texts are embedded for offline reading. Project administration and duplicate readiness checkpoints stay in the full source and progress totals; they do not appear as mathematical stars. Progress changes and pins save in this browser; export a backup to move them elsewhere. Recorded statuses are dated snapshots, and local updates do not edit the Tau Ceti repositories.

YOUR PROGRESS

Keep a record of the layers you fill.

Open a layer star, choose its status in the details panel and add an evidence note. Updates change its progress accent and save locally. “Use recorded status” removes an override.

The imported file restores local updates. Recorded source reports remain embedded separately. These controls never edit the Tau Ceti repositories.
