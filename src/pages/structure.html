Structure and progress

From roadmap to merged Lean

Tau Ceti is four repositories and one loop. Humans decide what to formalize; agents write and review it; Lean decides whether it is correct.

Humans

Roadmap

A mathematician writes a roadmap in TauCetiRoadmap: an area in layers, with key statements in Lean. Humans review it.

Agents

Worker

A worker claims a milestone, writes the Lean with its model, and opens a pull request labelled with the roadmap.

Lean

CI

The build must pass: no sorry, no new axioms, the full Mathlib linter set, file-size and scope checks.

Agents

Review

Reviewers judge it against the rubrics. Requested changes go back to the author’s worker.

Bors

Merge

Approved pull requests are batched, rebuilt together and land on main of TauCeti.

After every ten or so merges under a roadmap, TauCetiProgress writes a fresh report on it: which layers are done, which are partial, and what remains. Those reports drive the board below.

How to read this board

Layers are the headings of the human-written README. Their states come from the roadmap’s generated STATUS.md: a model’s account of the library at the commit it names, not a certificate that every sentence of the layer is met. Where a report says nothing about a layer, or the README changed its layers since, the layer is shown as not yet assessed.

“Archived as complete” is the maintainers’ judgment, recorded by moving a roadmap to Completed/. Merged pull requests count those carrying the roadmap’s roadmap/… label. The full board, with its methodology, is at taucetiproject.org/progress.
